JobsDone

We apply. You interview.

Looking for a job takes hours every day. Reading postings. Filling out the same forms again and again. JobsDone does that part for you.

How it works

  1. We set you up. We walk you through getting your own Muse account, step by step. Muse is a helper program that does the work on your own computer account. Your password stays yours — we never see it.
  2. You answer some questions. About the work you have done and the job you want. We clean up your resume and build your profile from your answers. No resume? No problem — we build one from your answers.
  3. Jobs get applied for, every weekday morning. Your helper searches and applies for jobs that fit your rules — your pay, your hours, your kind of work. You get one short summary every day: what was applied for, what came back, and anything that needs you.

You stay in charge

  • Anything that needs your OK waits for the times you pick. No surprises at random hours.
  • Some steps are always yours: proving you are not a robot on a website, signing forms, and the interviews. We never do those for you.
  • We can't promise you a job. No honest service can. We promise the work gets done — every fitting job, applied for, every weekday.

Who it works for

Any field. It was proven first in tech support, customer service, and office and admin work, including healthcare intake jobs.

Price

$100, one time. That includes the full setup, done with you step by step.

Your Muse account is separate — you pay Meta a monthly fee for it, like a phone bill. We will tell you the exact cost before you start, and we never touch that account or its password.

Refunds: Full refund any time before the work starts. Once the work starts, there is no refund — but everything made for you (your profile, your cleaned-up resume, your pack) is yours to keep. If we can't finish your setup, you get a full refund.

Your private information

  • We never ask for your Social Security number, birth date, passwords, or bank or card numbers.
  • Your information is never sold or shared.
  • Jobs are applied for from your own accounts, on your own Muse. When we are done, your helper is set up to keep working for you.
